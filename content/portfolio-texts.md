# Portfolio content

Edit published copy in its page rather than keeping parallel drafts here:

- `index.html`: a concise introduction with concrete technical substance and routes into the portfolio.
- `about.html`: a selective personal narrative about current work, the broader career, technical judgement and curiosity. Keep employers in project/CV headings and leave private anecdotes out.
- `projects.html`: selected work, explaining the need, personal contribution, decisions and outcome.
- `contact.html`: a personal introduction and the established contact links.
- `resume.html`: the CV interface and static fallback; interactive career data lives in `assets/data/`.

Let technical judgement show through the way Joakim considers dependencies and the effects of changes. Do not use architecture labels in the page copy. Show career breadth without turning the personal narrative into an employer list. Sales was an early professional strength; deeper software development came later. Early website building must not imply that every later programming skill was already present. Preserve the site's personal character without reducing the career to one employer, technology or job title.

Use the owner's explicit corrections over older copy. Existing CV dates, titles and achievements are starting points to check, not independently verified facts. Distinguish official titles from responsibilities, personal contributions from team or company results, and delivered work from plans. Do not invent metrics or attribute an entire company's growth to one person.

Public, general descriptions of bank responsibilities and working practices are permitted. Do not publish internal assignments or project descriptions, test or deployment environments, LPAR details, adoption figures, privileged-access details or strategy plans. Do not include private family information or raw interview notes. Use public general language without inventing a sanitized project or result.

Keep claims consistent across the page text, CV, metadata and sharing image. Contact destinations remain the established email, GitHub and LinkedIn links.

## Environments

Projects uses an original first-person industrial scene with a pause-menu-style project selector. About uses four linked rooms on a dungeon automap alongside the reading journal. Let the scene, materials and navigation convey the genres; keep the personal writing ordinary. Do not add game titles, fake skill statistics, character roles, mission language or mandatory game controls. All actual content must remain directly accessible through labelled links.
