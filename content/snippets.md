# Copy guidance

Use short, concrete descriptions in the first person. Prefer examples to slogans.

Open broadly with how Joakim connects technology and business. Let the selected work and CV carry technology lists and evidence. Lead with what Joakim built, decided, sold, organized or improved. Let specific work establish technical depth and career breadth. Explain responsibility and results without vague claims of doing everything, comparisons that dismiss colleagues, or unverified superlatives. Do not make constant overwork part of the professional offer.

Use active verbs for responsibilities supported by the interview; do not turn leading or building a function into merely helping. Keep official titles separate from descriptions of work. Use verbs that reflect the contribution accurately: building, implementing, coordinating, advising and contributing are different responsibilities. Avoid turning plans into achievements or company results into personal guarantees.

Live page text is in the five main HTML pages; CV translations and variants are in `assets/data/`. Follow `portfolio-texts.md` and `resume-texts.md` for publication boundaries and factual corrections. Do not maintain alternative career claims, duplicate drafts or private project details in this file.

## Voice

Use an understated first-person voice with room for the reader to infer. Convey depth through the way Joakim thinks about dependencies, tradeoffs and consequences, without naming architecture or assigning seniority labels. Avoid a running list of employers in personal narrative; use “på mitt nuvarande jobb” where appropriate. Company names belong in project and CV headings. Keep CV facts useful and clear.

The interview is background for writing, not a transcript to publish. Omit intimate family and childhood anecdotes, heroic incident-response claims and exhaustive career or gaming histories. Let the interface carry its references; do not explain the nostalgia, the nickname or the game versions. Keep the language relaxed and lightly suggestive without cryptic slogans or an invented hacker persona.
